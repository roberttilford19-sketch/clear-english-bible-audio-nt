# Clear English Bible — New Testament Audio

This shelf holds the New Testament half of the **Clear Translation Bible**, read aloud: 27 books, from Matthew to Revelation.

The Clear Translation Bible is a clear-English modernization of the public-domain World English Bible Classic, checked against that base verse by verse. It is not a fresh translation from the Greek, and it does not claim to be. The readings are DRAFT, awaiting the editor's read-through, and are offered freely for listening.

**Editor:** Robert Tilford.
**Reader:** Isabella — the voice of Awen, the editor's AI assistant. AI assistance in the preparation of this work is disclosed plainly, here and everywhere the work appears.

Every book on this shelf stands whole, in one file each — no New Testament book needed dividing. (On the Old Testament shelf, eight long books stand in two parts each, so that every file fits; nothing is cut out.)

The text of the whole Bible stands on its own shelf: **clear-translation-bible**. The Old Testament readings stand on the companion audio shelf: **clear-translation-bible-audio-ot**. This shelf is **clear-translation-bible-audio-nt**.

Every file on this shelf is listed, with its size and length, in `MANIFEST.csv`. In all, this shelf carries about 21 hours of listening.

The shelves by their settled names: the text of the whole Bible stands at [Clear English Bible](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/clear-english-bible); the Old Testament readings stand at [Clear English Bible — Old Testament Audio](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/clear-english-bible-audio-ot); this shelf is [Clear English Bible — New Testament Audio](https://github.com/roberttilford19-sketch/clear-english-bible-audio-nt).
